Chad Douglas Lewine, born 1989 in Montgomeryville, PA. 36 years old. An artist whose catalog — 151 songs across 16 release cycles from 2010 to the present — functions less as a body of work than as a longitudinal instrument for recording what a refusing-to-comply nervous system produces when placed inside a culture designed to extract compliance. Works in music (primary), visual art as CHADDYD (parallel band), and writing (temporarily withdrawn, will return as books). Operates from a thesis he's articulated publicly for years: that pop music is the most infectious mind-implant technology humans have engineered, and that the tradition of using it for consciousness elevation — spirituals to troubadours to Marvin Gaye to Marley to Michael Jackson — was interrupted, not ended, by the streaming/algorithmic era. He is working to restore it. That is the mission. Everything else — the site, the Rising Compass, the live show, the 151 songs each treated as a permanent gold-bar asset — is the infrastructure the mission requires.
