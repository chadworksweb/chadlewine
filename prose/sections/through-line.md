A body began reporting in childhood, found music as its primary report format, refused every label the machine tried to pin to it, survived every capture point, and is now building the door that almost didn't exist for the next person whose body is doing the same thing.

Born into a machine that medicates, rehabs, courts, algorithmically flattens, tone-polices, and stamps every person it processes — Chad passed through every one of those capture points and came out with the map instead of the brand. The catalog is the record of what got made while that refusal was ongoing. The Two Roads document is the retroactive articulation of what the refusal was actually accomplishing.

Every label the culture tried to make stick — aspiring pop star, starving artist, cringe, satire, too much, too intense, too direct, too loud, addict, offender, disordered, off-brand — failed to stick. The refusal of labels is the actual arc.
