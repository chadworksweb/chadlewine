Move to Brooklyn June 2011 for an SEO job in Midtown Manhattan. Three months later, the job is done — and freelance web design becomes the permanent economic substrate from that day forward.

**The Human Link (May 2011)** releases almost simultaneously with the move. Compass avg +18.5. The album's shape as a record is still pop-conventional in places (*Life is a Ride*, *Catch Me* both Degraded) but *Michael* is there at +78, and *Ask & Tell* at +76. And *T.G.A. (The Gay Anthem)* at +38 Elevated is already one of the defining tracks of his early voice — a coming-out statement, yes, but also an assertion that being gay doesn't mean any of the things the culture would try to reduce it to. *"It doesn't mean I can't rap / It doesn't mean I have the clap / Or HIV man, open up your eyes and see."* Refusal of labels. At 21.

**Williamsburgadelphia (April 2012)** is the only album in the catalog with a negative compass average (−4.2). This is a five-track EP, four tracks read Decent or Degraded, driven by *'Til I Pop* (−33, ego/fame content). I'd read this as the period where the NYC grind, the starving-artist transition, the club scene, and the distance from formation are all dragging the signal down hardest. It's not a fall. It's the closest the catalog ever comes to matching industry-era convention. And then he corrects.

**Life as a Student (August 2013)** with SubKrim producing. Compass avg +15.5. Consistent mid-range Decent-to-Elevated. *Ancient Flow* specifically is worth flagging: it's the earliest explicit articulation of the lineage thesis. "I'm on that ancient flow, pyramid / Let the Mayans build it / Pompeii, Egypt knew the secret / We're here to release it." Compass +18, Decent, but read as biographical data this is Chad at 23 already naming that he's in a tradition that predates him. The ancient-lineage pillar isn't a 2026 reconstruction. It's been in the lyrics since 2013.

**HoneyChrome / The Chocolate Album (September 2013)** is the first self-produced album. Compass avg +7.7 — mostly Decent. This is the period he describes descending into the NYC/Brooklyn rave scene with "a steady supply of MDMA and both mainstream and underground raves every weekend." Lyrically the album is more club-oriented and less mission-forward; the compass reads it accurately. But the artifact itself matters: self-produced, paired with 8 truffles from baker friend Travis Tribolet in Pittsburgh, only a handful physically made. The first move toward treating the album-as-object rather than album-as-content. Early gesture toward what would later become the whole site architecture.
