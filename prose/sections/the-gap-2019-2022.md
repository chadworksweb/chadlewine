Moves to his mom's house October 2019, stays until March 2020 when Covid hits and the environment becomes untenable. Moves to Rochester to live near Janet — finally her web designer, the carrot on the stick for years.

**This is the era of the songs in the head.** 24/7 intrusive musical inner monologue, louder than the TV. Three years of it, then two more years of it converting to tension headaches, then resolution in late 2024. The Two Roads document maps this era in its fullest articulation. The body was reporting. Every exit except alcohol had been sealed. Janet had convinced him she was "1000 times better than any doctor or therapist." He drank half a bottle of Jameson every night to suppress the reporting enough to sleep.

The catalog of RELEASED songs from this period is less dense than you'd expect given the life intensity, because many of these songs are demos, and because some of what he wrote during the cult period got left behind when he left it. **The Gap** album releases August 2021, after he's already left Rochester and the cult (November 2020) and moved back to the Philly suburbs (February 2021). Compass avg +38.4 — a dramatic rise from Feeling High's +12. *Freedom Ring* at +76 addresses Trump-era national fracture directly. *Higher Ground* at +78 is the activist-protest track. *Fool's Gold Kingdom* at +42 is explicitly about leaving the cult: "Twenty years of living under ruthless rule / Blinded by fake wisdom, I was just a tool / Just lived for you, thought your word was true / That I had been given a golden spoon." *Breaking the Bond* at +38 is the first of what the doc describes as "many of my songs that address narcissistic and emotionally abusive relationships." *Radiate (Piano Demo)* at +76 is a serene survival statement. *Magic* at +76 closes with "magic isn't lost, it's inside of me."

The Gap is, in charge terms, the first album where the Ascended tier becomes dominant rather than exceptional. The figure is rising literally, in the compass data, at the exact moment he identifies in the doc as the reset.

Then Lansdale 2021–2022: Scott, cocaine, the arrest, the end of the relationship with his mother. The doc details this with clinical precision. The narcissistic-abuse-survival album — "an album's worth of songs" he wrote during this period that "would resonate with a lot of people" — sits in demo state. These songs are part of the arc but not yet part of the public catalog. They're real work; they're just not produced-and-released work yet. State is a property of the song entity; the demos are arc-visible by their state, not excluded from it.

The single *35* (June 2022) comes out of this period. Compass reads Decent +8 — the content is literal ("I wanna buy a house by 35") and the compass takes it at that level. But the doc frames this song as the moment he realized apartment living wasn't working for him and he needed a permanent home. That realization is a survival marker. The compass can't read survival markers directly. It reads what's on the page. On the page, *35* is a guy who wants a house. In the life, *35* is a 32-year-old who has just been arrested, lost his mother, and is wanting — for the first time — something material that would mean he couldn't be displaced again.
