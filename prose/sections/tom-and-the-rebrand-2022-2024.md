November 2022: moves one town over to North Wales. Three weeks later meets Tom. Doc: "He is the only person currently in my life besides my father that fully gets me, and I would not be making this video, let alone pursuing Chad Rising at all, if I did not have him as a pillar in my life."

*Dark Nights* releases February 2023. Compass reads Elevated +52. Here's where the write-date / release-date dual reading matters: the song was written in 2013 in NYC about his best friend Chad, who had relapsed into addiction. Released in 2023 — a decade later — by a Chad who has himself come through the cocaine years, the alcohol years, the arrest, and is now on the other side. Same lyric, doing different work in two moments of his life. Written as witness to a friend collapsing. Released as survivor-to-survivor address. Both are the song. The arc's dual-time model handles this cleanly.

Early 2023: Paul Edward McBride, Liverpool songwriter, makes contact. Mentorship begins. Tom funds part of it. Year-long songwriter bootcamp. **April 2024: rebrand as Chad Rising.** Enter short-form social media.

December 2023 – late 2024: runs the @creed_takemehigher fan account up to ~10k followers. Strategy suggested by Paul: build a fan community for a known band, then create music that community would like. The strategy would work, Chad writes, but "the majority of Creed fans aren't going to vibe with the real me, and I was not willing to fake that I vibed with them." Another refusal-of-label moment. 200 of those 10k follow him over to his Chad Rising account.

**Pivotal Days (November 2024)** is the album that emerges. Compass avg +56.8 — the highest album average in the entire catalog. *The Divide's A Lie* (+78), *Pirate's Life* (+76), *How Do You Wanna Feel* (+76), *Eye For An Eye* (+76), *Pivotal Days* (+76). The album is mostly written for leased professional instrumentals, which makes him prolific for the first time since the Brooklyn years. The title itself — "pivotal days" — is a frame he's now operating from openly: we are in them, humanity is in them, the songs are dispatches from inside them. This is also the first era where the charge data and the persona name align. "Chad Rising" is the artist. The catalog is, quantifiably, rising. The compass didn't know about the rebrand; it read the work. The work is rising. Charge and identity have fused.
