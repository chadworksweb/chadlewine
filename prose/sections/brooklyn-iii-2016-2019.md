**Feeling High (August 2017)** — compass avg +12.0. A dip from SPROUT's +38.8. This is the album written during the onset of the Bed Stuy / Project Parlor / cocaine-as-regular-thing era, and the compass reading tracks the life data: *White Lightning* at −32 Degraded, *Boomerang (Demo)* Decent −6, *Cashing Out* and *See Through Me* both Decent +8 (explicit anxiety/depression/financial-panic songs — *"I don't wanna feel like this no more, no / Don't wanna feel like I'm poor no more, no more"*). But *Finding Freedom* at +38 and *I Work Hard Everyday* at +28 are still doing mission work. The album is him descending and holding the line at the same time. Compass reads Decent-heavy because the tracks are emotionally accurate to where he was, which was: struggling, not elevating, not giving up.

May 2017: the reconnection with a 6-years-earlier fling, the falling "deeply, madly in love," and the abrupt non-return of feelings. Doc: "I cried the entire flight and taxi ride home. It was embarrassing, but I couldn't control the tears. I had never felt that before and haven't felt it since." He names this as the ripping-open of the wound of never having been loved unconditionally — the wound that the mother pattern would later explicitly confirm. This event accelerates the downward spiral.

*Protector* from Feeling High is interesting in retrospect: compass reads Decent +12, but the doc identifies it as "spiritually or romantically ambiguous." It might be reading at Decent because the compass can't tell whether the "protector" is human or divine. The ambiguity is the point. In the lineage of songs Chad has been writing to a never-arriving figure (romantic, paternal, divine, communal), *Protector* sits somewhere in the middle of the scale. When Tom appears in 2022, the Protector role finally lands in a real human. But that's five years away.

**Boomerang (single, September 2018)** with Mike Rogers — the vocal producer of "Groove is in the Heart" — extracting and refining Chad's signature vocal character. Compass reads Decent −8 because the content is romantic-pop, not mission-forward; but the CRAFT leap is real and the doc frames this as the emergence of the vocal identity that still defines him. "A year of pushing Boomerang to no avail" then leads to the stop-making-music / focus-on-songwriting decision, roughly 2019.

October 1, 2019: leaves Brooklyn, four days before his 30th birthday. The doc names this as appropriately timed.

**The thread of Janet the guru** runs through this entire Brooklyn decade, intensifying over time. She is the long-distance spiritual authority figure he is in contact with for "8 or so years" while he is in NYC. The doc frames it in retrospect as grooming, brainwashing, cult prep.
