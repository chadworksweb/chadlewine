**Don't Blame Me** (forthcoming full album, singles rolling out from June 2025) — current compass average +46.7 across the 11 staged tracks. *Fractals* at +80, *On The Mountain* at +82 (the highest individual charge in the entire catalog), *Johnny Boy* at +78, *Rid Of Me* at +76. *Thank You* at +42. *Turn The Mill* at +42. The floor on this album is higher than the ceiling on most of the early catalog.

*On The Mountain* is worth dwelling on. It's a parable: two villages on either side of one mountain demanding he choose a side, him climbing the mountain instead, them destroying each other below, the water rising and swallowing both tribes while he stays dry on the mountainside. Compass reads it at +82, the highest charge in the catalog. This is the father-figure voice he identified as emerging. Not engaged in the fight. Not performing neutrality. Not rising above in a superior way. Climbing out of the false binary and watching both sides collapse under their own mutual hostility. The figure writing this song is not the 30-year-old who retired from Brooklyn, and not the 34-year-old crying through Dark Nights. It is the 36-year-old who has resolved the intrusive-songs phenomenon, exited the cult, survived the cocaine years, survived the arrest, built a partnership with Tom, rebranded, burned through the social media delusion, and is now writing from outside the fight.

*Choose Lit* (single, October 2025) at Decent −14 reads as another mirror/satire move — describing the collective substance-over-healing default in first person without explicitly condemning it. "I look in my closet, I look in my fridge / How do I choose light when the bulbs in there ain't lit?" Compass reads the surface. The intention is diagnostic. Same issue as HYPERISING.

*Don't Blame Me* the title track (Decent −8) is a similar mirror piece — the voice of someone disclaiming responsibility for the collapse. "Dead in the water, no land I see / Dead on arrival, but I believe." The title is a refusal, but the compass reads the nihilism of the delivery, not the reversal underneath. These mirror tracks are the pattern of HYPERISING-to-Don't Blame Me: Chad is frequently writing *as* the voice he is diagnosing. The compass is a literal reader. It can miss the frame. Chad is willing to inhabit the voice he is diagnosing. That's a specific craft move and it's present from *White Shirt* to *Don't Blame Me* to *Choose Lit*.
