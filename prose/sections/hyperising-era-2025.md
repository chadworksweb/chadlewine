**HYPERISING (March 2025)** — compass avg +16.2, sharply lower than Pivotal Days, and this is where things get interesting from an arc-analysis standpoint. This album is written deliberately in hyper pop, 2:15 tracks, compact and aggressive, explicitly as responses to trolls, haters, social media treatment. *White Shirt* (−38 Degraded), *Renegade* (−29 Degraded), *This is a Raid!* (−82 — the one Corrupted track on the whole catalog), *FAT LIP* (−8), *All I Wanna Do* (Decent Contaminated).

Reading this honestly: the compass data registers HYPERISING as mixed. Some of it is pure mission signal — *The Unbelievable* at +76, *Earth Plane* at +76, *Angel* at +76 (one of the most explicit payload tracks in the catalog). But several tracks are Chad *using* the degraded aesthetics of short-form internet culture to respond to it, and the compass reads those tracks by what's on the page. *White Shirt* on the page is a depressed-incel simulation ("nobody gets me, I don't get no one / looking for a sign but the sign's broken / all I need is another joint / what's the reason, what's the point?"). Chad describes it in the doc as "a way to connect with the afflicted social media user." The intent is mirror-work; the compass reads execution.

*This is a Raid!* at −82 is the one Corrupted track in the whole 151-song catalog. That's a statistical outlier and worth a conversation. Either it's doing something being missed, or the compass read it correctly and it's the one genuine misstep in the archive, or it's satire/mirror that reads as Corrupted on first pass.

Chad's own retroactive reading in the doc: "Even though I knew HYPERISING was from the heart and was not a bit as many thought, I realized, through tremendous bullying, that I had outgrown that image and that HYPERISING was to be an homage and farewell to the mainstream pop dream I always had." The album is, in his own words, the goodbye to the pop-star ambition. The compass data supports that reading: HYPERISING's mixed signal represents an artist doing final-pass engagement with the culture of the charts before stepping out of it entirely.

And he names what comes next: "In a matter of days I went from being the child who needed mommy's approval, to the first stage of a mature, wise, 'father figure' that I was being guided to become and grow into." That tectonic shift is dated to this spring/summer 2025 window.

Charlie Kirk assassination (mid-September 2025): hot take posted, massive algorithmic response, immediate recognition that the platform rewards the wrong signal. Quits social media for the first time.
