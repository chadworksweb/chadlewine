Writing lyrics on any scrap paper at the Kay Jewelers counter between customers. Boyfriend Vince supportive. Posts on Craigslist for recording help, finds Izzy the hobbyist engineer, cuts rudimentary tracks rapping over beats from Mario, Sean Kingston, Lil Wayne, Kid Cudi. Takes the name Chad D. Moves into first apartment in Highland Park, learns Ableton quickly, produces **Demoesque** — released March 2010 — as his first full original release. Thirty-odd tracks ranging from *Grab a Pack* (a letter to his mom about smoking blunts) to *Applause* (early-career proclamation) to *Michael* (already: a song about the suicide of a college friend).

Compass reading on Demoesque: average charge +12.9, with a bimodal distribution. The degraded tracks are the club-escapist / substance-positive / ego-proclamation songs (*Coming To Get Ya* −32, *Grab A Pack* −38, *Chase Me* −28, *The Human Link Demo* −30). The elevated tracks are the mission signal already coming through (*Planet Worth* +76, *Michael (Demo)* +78, *Kitty Hawk* +28, *Family* +38). This split is characteristic and important: the mission was present from the first release, coexisting with the pop conventions of the moment. A twenty-year-old Chad writing both *Grab A Pack* and *Planet Worth* on the same record is the figure already fully formed, just not yet sorted.

Two things from Demoesque that deserve attention in the arc:

*Planet Worth* at +76 is a ceiling-level Ascended track on his very first release. It's evidence that the spec was intact from day one. The system doesn't have to be learned; it has to be defended.

*Michael* — the song about his college friend's suicide — hits +78 on the Ascended tier and would be re-recorded and released again on The Human Link in 2011. The lyric: "no one saw it coming, he got tired of running / running from something no one can define / only answer to the puzzle is locked up in his mind." That's a 20-year-old already writing from inside the frame that the Two Roads document is built on — a body screaming, unseen, discharging, ending. The machine of labels hadn't caught him yet, and he was already writing about the cost of it not catching his friend.

New Hope, PA connection via Dwayne Dunlevy → Anthony Krizan (Spin Doctors, Lenny Kravitz). First studio single *Life is a Ride* recorded at Sonic Boom Studios, Raritan NJ. This is the first song on a "real" album (The Human Link, 2011) and compass-reads as Degraded at −28. It's the club escapism track. Compass call is accurate. It's also the song that the mainstream industry almost caught him with — this was the commercial-viability moment. Doc: "Had I been sucked in to the major label world, either with Life is a Ride or during this time, I never would have become who I needed to be."
